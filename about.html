Our Story & Sourcing Philosophy

Founded with a passion for community traditions, North Star Bakery began as a small family project to bring traditional sourdough methods back to our neighborhood. We believe in using pure local ingredients, sustainable flour sourcing, and slow baking techniques that respect the natural process of artisan dough handling.

Meet Our Dedicated Team

Our bakers arrive long before dawn every single morning to ensure fresh, warm treats are ready for your daily commute. We care about fostering connections, supporting local schools, and making our kitchen an open place of comfort for everyone.
